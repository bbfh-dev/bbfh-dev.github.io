Minecraft maps

CrowScout I (former Hey Neighbor I)

CrowScout I is a survival horror stealth Minecraft 1.19.4 map inspired by the Hello Neighbor franchise for two players. You can get it on PlanetMinecraft!

It took many months of development, I was in charge of game design, game logic, building the map. I got a lot of help from Taizaii: she built the majority of the interiors.

This map does have some unresolved issues including problems with game design, but I haven't got to fix them.
