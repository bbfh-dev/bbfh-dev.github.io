CrowScout I (former Hey Neighbor I)

May of 2023
External Download
CrowScout I is a survival horror stealth Minecraft 1.19.4 map inspired by the Hello Neighbor franchise for two players — PlanetMinecraft.

A final piece in my Hello Neighbor inspired map series. It combines all experience I've built over the years and all ideas I had from my previous maps.

The most complete map I've ever released, however it still has many known issues I will not resolve since I quit working on Minecraft projects.

If there is one map I want someone to play it would be this one.

Hello Neighbor 2.1 [1.15.2] [1.14.4]

April of 2020
External Download
Translated from Russian: Map based on the game Hello Neighbor. On this map, the neighbor (the second player) can put cameras and traps. He needs to keep the player away from the basement. The player's task is to get into the basement! — Minecraft-Inside (Russian site)

I didn't really speak English back then, so I developed maps in Russian. This map features doors with locks, cameras that detect when player passes by, and a pretty simple game logic.

Just like all next maps it is not going to be as fun to play, however it's an important part of my map development history as I was experimenting with various gameplay features.

Hello BAV I Duo [1.15.2] [1.14.4]

January of 2020
External Download
Translated from Russian: This is a map for two people. It's like Hello Neighbor in Minecraft. It has dreams, puzzles and doors that need to be unlocked. — Minecraft-Inside (Russian site)

This map had an interesting concept (although not new to the Hello Neighbor video game) of having dreams that you have to complete to unlock stuff. It also features a really big house making it pretty hard to play for both neighbor and player.

Hey Neighbor [1.15.2] [1.14.4]

January of 2020
External Download
Translated from Russian: On this map you can see the amazing story of a family who had a friendly neighbor, but something went wrong.... Will you be able to cope and get into the basement? It's a two-player map — Minecraft-Inside (Russian site)

Since this map has a lot of dialogue, you will not be able to enjoy it unless you understand the Russian language.

The special part about this map, is that I implemented cutscenes to showcase the plot. The problem is that during those cutscenes the other player is... oh well... in the middle of nowhere.
